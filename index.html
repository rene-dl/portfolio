About

Connecting business needs with data-driven, technology-enabled solutions.

I'm a Data Analyst / Business Intelligence professional with experience designing, building and maintaining analytical ecosystems inside banking environments — where the data has to be right and the decisions built on it carry real weight.

My work sits at the intersection of business intelligence, data analytics, SQL and data modeling on one side, and automation and low-code application development on the other. I spend most of my time doing three things: understanding what a business area actually needs, shaping the data so it can answer that need, and building the dashboard, application or automation that puts the answer in someone's hands.

That usually means modeling a data warehouse, writing and tuning SQL, designing a Power BI dashboard people will actually open every morning, or building a Power Platform application that removes a manual step from someone's week.

Business Intelligence · Data Analytics

BI Dashboards for Banking Operations

01

Problem

Different business areas — deposits, credit, cards, HR, sales force — tracked performance manually or through scattered spreadsheets, making it slow and inconsistent to see how the business was actually performing.

02

Solution

Built a family of Power BI dashboards on top of scalable star and snowflake data models: executive KPIs, operational drill-downs, dynamic filters, geographic and product-level breakdowns, customer segmentation, goal tracking, sales hierarchies and row-level security so each area sees only what it should.

03

Impact

Gave each business area a single, trusted source of truth for daily decisions, replacing manual reporting cycles with dashboards that update on their own and scale to new areas without rebuilding the data model.

Power BI SQL DAX Data Modeling RLS

Business Applications · Automation

Incident Management Platform

01

Problem

The sales force had no structured way to report and track incidents involving enterprise and government clients, so cases got lost between teams and follow-up depended on email threads and memory.

02

Solution

Built a Power Apps platform to register, classify and monitor incidents by case number, with dynamic filters, an internal chat with the responsible team, file management, action buttons and automated routing to the right area — with room to integrate into external systems such as Remedy.

03

Impact

Gave every incident a single owner and a visible status from report to resolution. The platform was a finalist for "Most Visionary System" in an internal innovation awards program.

Power Apps Power Automate Dataverse SharePoint

Business Applications · Automation

Commercial Offers App

01

Problem

Special commercial offers were tracked informally, so it was hard to know which offers were still valid, who was responsible for them, and whether their terms were actually being met.

02

Solution

Built a Power Apps application on Dataverse to register offers, track validity periods and compliance metrics, and used Power Automate to trigger alerts and reminders as expiration dates approach.

03

Impact

Gave the commercial team visibility into every active offer and its compliance status, cutting down on offers that expired unnoticed or were misapplied.

Data Analytics · Commercial Analytics

Pharmacy Sales Analytics

01

Problem

Raw point-of-sale data existed, but there was no clear read on which products, categories and pricing strategies were actually driving growth and margin.

02

Solution

Extracted, cleaned and transformed sales data with SQL and Python, then analyzed total sales, growth, average ticket, top-performing products, margin by category and inventory turnover, and built an interactive Tableau dashboard on top of it.

03

Impact

Surfaced concrete pricing and inventory opportunities the pharmacy could act on immediately, backed by a dashboard the team could keep using after the analysis was delivered.
